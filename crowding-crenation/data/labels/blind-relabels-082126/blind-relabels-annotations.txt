Blind re-annotation set -- worksheet
====================================

50 FOVs, filenames randomised. Slide identity, FOV number and the previous labels are
deliberately not recoverable from this folder -- that is the point, so please do not go
looking for them before annotating.

One line per image. The id matches the .png filename, so fov-07 below is fov-07.png.
Write the tags after the colon, comma-separated:

  density (pick exactly one):
      No Cells        nothing to judge -- blank field, or debris only
      Few Cells       cells countable one by one; too few to have a packing regime
      Sparser         a real but thin monolayer; packing is judgeable
      Monolayer | Slightly Dense | Dense | Very Dense
  overlap (optional; omit for none. Not allowed on No Cells):
      Slight Rouleaux | Some Rouleaux | Rouleaux | Heavy Rouleaux
  quality (optional, repeatable):
      Crenated | Unfocused | Overexposed | Artifact | Other Dimples

Examples:  fov-07: Slightly Dense, Some Rouleaux, Crenated
           fov-08: Few Cells, Slight Rouleaux
           fov-09: No Cells

The density question to ask yourself is "can I judge how packed this field is?"
Nothing there at all -> No Cells. Countable one by one -> Few Cells. Thin but genuine
monolayer -> Sparser.

NOTE: fov-01 and fov-02 below say "very rouleaux", which is not one of the four overlap
levels. Heavy Rouleaux is probably what was meant -- left as written rather than changed.
